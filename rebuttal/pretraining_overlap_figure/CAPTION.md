**MorphEM-specific pretraining-overlap exclusion sensitivity.** Points show MorphEM minus each comparator in the Raw PA × PC score; horizontal bars are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 50,000 paired cluster-bootstrap replicates. Positive values favor MorphEM. **(a)** Excluding 12,132 directly matched held-out wells retained 109,790 wells. **(b)** Excluding every held-out well on a MorphEM-matched plate retained 74,137 wells. All six displayed contrasts remained supported after a separate 21-comparison Holm adjustment within each subset. OpenPhenom is included only as a comparator: no OpenPhenom-specific exclusion was constructed because its released documentation provides no image- or plate-level membership manifest. Normalized profiles and selected Raw recipes were fixed rather than refitted, and the exclusions alter task composition; the figure therefore does not estimate a causal effect of pretraining exposure or establish a leakage-free evaluation.
